GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to plan a relaxed visit to California Republic, St Ives.

What style of food do you serve?

Our menu is inspired by California, bringing together Western favourites, Mexican classics and creative fusion flavours. From seasonal bowls, steaks and ribs to fish and chips, tacos, burritos and quesadillas, there’s plenty to explore—all in a relaxed, welcoming setting.

Do I need to make a booking?

We recommend booking ahead, especially for weekends and special occasions. Book a table online, or call us. For large groups and functions, please make an inquiry or give us a call to discuss your plans.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Of course! Walk-ins are always welcome, subject to table availability. We recommend booking ahead, especially on weekends, public holidays or for larger groups, so we can arrange a suitable table and have everything set up for your arrival.

How can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

If your plans change, please let us know as early as possible. For urgent changes, give us a call. If you’d prefer to visit another day, we’ll be happy to help reschedule your booking, subject to availability.

Can I book for a birthday or a group celebration?

Yes! We’d love to help you celebrate! Book a table for your birthday meal and enjoy a complimentary birthday dessert. For larger groups or functions, please make an inquiry so we can help with the arrangements.

Can I bring my own cake?

Yes! You’re welcome to bring your own cake, with no cakeage fee. We also offer a complimentary dessert for birthday celebrations—just let our team know you’re celebrating.

Can I bring my own decorations?

Yes! You’re welcome to bring table decorations and place cards and set them up yourself. Please let us know in your booking notes or give us a call beforehand so we can arrange a suitable time for you to set up.

Are you family-friendly?

Absolutely! We have a kids’ menu and colouring activities to keep little ones entertained while you enjoy a relaxed family meal.

Do you have high chairs?

Yes! Please ask a team member when you arrive, or add a request to your booking notes so we can prepare one for you.

Is there wheelchair access?

Yes! Please let us know in your booking notes if you need space for a wheelchair, so we can arrange a suitable table and make room before you arrive.

Can you accommodate allergies and dietary requests?

Yes! We offer a wide range of gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan and nut-free options. However, our menu contains allergens, and food is prepared in a shared kitchen that handles gluten, nuts and other allergens. While we make every reasonable effort to accommodate dietary needs, we cannot guarantee that any dish will be 100% allergen-free. Please let our team know about any allergies or dietary requirements before ordering.

Do you allow BYO wine or alcohol?

We don’t currently offer BYO wine or alcohol, but we have an extensive drinks menu with plenty to enjoy alongside your meal. Take a look at our drinks menu before your visit. If our BYO policy changes, we’ll update this page to let you know.

Do you offer takeaway?

Yes! Browse our takeaway menu and order online for collection from California Republic, St Ives. Alternatively, give us a call to place a takeaway order.

Where can I find your lunch specials and happy hour offers?

Explore our lunch specials and happy hour offers for the latest menus, prices and serving times.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes! There are plenty of parking options nearby. Council parking outside and around the restaurant includes 1-, 2- and 4-hour spaces during the day, with unlimited parking after 5pm. The nearby shopping centre also offers 3 hours of free parking. Please check the signs for the conditions that apply to your space.
